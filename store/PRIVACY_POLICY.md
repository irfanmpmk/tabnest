# TabNest Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

TabNest ("the extension") is a Chrome extension for managing tabs and saving tab sessions. This policy explains what data the extension handles and what it does with it.

## Summary

TabNest does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything stays in your browser on your device.

## Data the extension handles

To do its job, TabNest reads the **titles and URLs of your open tabs**. It uses them to:

- show and search your open tabs,
- find duplicate tabs and sort tabs,
- save tabs as sessions when you ask it to, and
- keep an automatic recovery copy of your open tabs (you can turn this off in Settings).

Saved sessions, the recovery copy and your settings are stored with Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API, which keeps them on your device only. Tabs in incognito windows are never saved.

Website icons are shown using Chrome's built-in favicon cache. TabNest does not contact websites to fetch them.

## What TabNest does not do

- It has no servers and makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- It does not sell, transfer or share data with anyone.
- It does not read the content of web pages.

## Your control over your data

- Delete individual sessions from the extension's popup.
- Delete all stored data from TabNest's Settings page ("Delete all sessions").
- Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from your browser.
- Exporting sessions creates a file on your computer that only you control.
